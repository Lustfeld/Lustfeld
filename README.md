## Julius Lustfeld

I am pursuing an MSc in Economic and Social Sciences at Bocconi University in Milan and
expect to graduate in March 2028. My coursework includes Time Series Analysis, Advanced
Statistics, Macroeconometrics, and Advanced Macroeconomics, and this year I am taking
Quantitative Portfolio Management and Financial Markets. Before Bocconi, I completed my
bachelor's degree in Economics at the University of Bayreuth.

I am mainly interested in exchange rates and interest rates, and in how the relationships
between markets change during periods of stress. In my courses I have worked with
state-space models and the Kalman filter, hidden Markov models, and Bayesian methods such
as Gibbs sampling, and I enjoy applying them to real financial data.

### Projects

**[safe-haven-dlm](https://github.com/Lustfeld/safe-haven-dlm)**

For my bachelor's thesis, which received the highest possible grade and a Deutsche
Bundesbank Special Award, I used R to examine how safe-haven currencies relate to equity
markets, US Treasury yields, and risk indicators across major crises. In this repository
I rebuilt the analysis in Python on free data and extended it with a dynamic linear
model, so that the relationships can change over time instead of staying fixed. The
analysis looks back at what happened and does not make forecasts.

### What I am working on now

For my master's thesis, I plan to build on this work and move from looking back to
forecasting in real time. I want to use Bayesian regime-switching models to study stress
in currency markets, and I will define the exact research question together with my
supervisor.

### Tools

I use R for most of my statistical work and am building up my Python skills, which is
where safe-haven-dlm comes in. I write in LaTeX and have first experience with Bloomberg
and Refinitiv.
